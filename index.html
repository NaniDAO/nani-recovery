Recover an account

If you set a guardian on a nani account and can no longer sign for it, the guardian can move everything out from here. You need the guardian's wallet and nothing else — not the Mac, not the recovery phrase.

1 The account you lost

Guardians are set per chain. If you set one on more than one chain, recover each separately.

2 Connect the guardian

The wallet you connect must be an owner of that account. Its signature is what authorises the recovery, and it pays the gas.

3 What to move, and where

An address you control and can still sign for. Check every character — this cannot be undone.

A token is missing

Discovery uses a public indexer, which can miss things. Balances are always read from the chain, never from the indexer.

4 Sign and submit

Finishing a recovery you started earlier

If the account had a waiting period, your recovery is queued rather than done. Come back when the wait is over and finish it here. Anyone can complete it — it runs exactly what was queued and nothing else.

Paste a saved recovery

There is no server here and nothing to sign up for. Everything on this page is read from the chain, and the transaction is signed by your own wallet — which will show you the same details independently before you approve them.
